01 / Challenge & role

Connect the brand promise to the purchase journey.

The ZOLEO website needed to serve people discovering satellite communication, shoppers comparing hardware, and Work Teams exploring the solution. Alongside a new storytelling direction, the introduction of ecommerce expanded the website’s role from product education to direct purchase.

The design challenge

Give different audiences a relevant path while creating a consistent experience across the website.

My responsibilities

Translate research and the design brief into page structure, content hierarchy, and primary actions for each audience and stage of the journey.

What I designed

Key page layouts and a new ecommerce PDP, guided by a shared visual direction and distinct page objectives.

01

Connect research, brand, and audience needs.

I analyzed US and Canadian performance across organic and paid traffic, using GA4, Search Console, and PageSpeed Insights. I also reviewed how ZOLEO and competitors appeared in AI-generated search comparisons. Together, these inputs informed priorities for page performance, opening-page clarity, and product education. MyZOLEO 2.0 alignment, the storytelling platform, and the brand book provided additional design inputs.

02

Create consistency without making every page the same.

The design review identified variation in button treatments, typography, and page patterns. This made consistency a priority alongside each page’s distinct objective: discovery, evaluation, or purchase.

03

Design a dedicated purchase experience.

With ecommerce new to the site, I designed the PDP around Add to Cart, supporting product information, plan comparison, and relevant accessories. Its purpose was to build purchase confidence at the final decision point.

The UX/UI opportunity

Make the website feel like one connected experience.

My design review focused on consistency across pages: button treatments, typographic emphasis, and the arrangement of content and next steps.

Buttons & calls to action

Design observation: Button treatments and the emphasis given to actions varied across pages.

Design response: Establish a more consistent button language and distinguish the primary action from supporting links, based on each page’s purpose.

Typography & hierarchy

Design observation: Heading treatments, text scale, and emphasis varied across pages.

Design response: Use a shared type hierarchy so headings, supporting copy, and product details have clearer roles.

Page structure & flow

Design observation: Pages used long feature sections and different layout patterns. I focused on grouping information and clarifying the next action.

Design response: Group related content, use more consistent spacing and section patterns, and align the next action with the visitor’s stage, from discovery to purchase.

04 / Design evolution

A clearer story, from discovery to purchase.

Selected before-and-after comparisons across the homepage, platform, hardware, app, and ecommerce product detail pages. Open any screen to explore it here, without leaving the case study.

The first impression

Lead with peace of mind. Help visitors find their path.

The objectiveIntroduce ZOLEO to both Adventurers and Work Teams, build an emotional connection, and guide each audience toward relevant content.

Design direction. The homepage direction puts the brand promise and Safety Circle first, with product capability supporting the story. Shopping, adventurer stories, and work-team content provide distinct next steps.

Platform overview

Explain the whole experience, one part at a time.

The objectiveHelp visitors new to the category understand ZOLEO as a complete Safety and Preparedness Platform.

Design direction. The overview leads with the emotional promise, then explains how the device, app, and network work together. Deeper device and app exploration supports the journey from curiosity to purchase consideration.

Understanding the product

Give informed visitors the detail to decide.

The objectiveHelp satellite-aware shoppers compare the hardware and move toward purchase.

Design direction. The hardware page prioritizes features, how the device works, specifications, and proof. Peace of mind is the reward those capabilities support, with Shop ZOLEO as the intended next step.

The companion app

Bring the app’s capabilities into focus.

The redesign introduces app features through a more consistent sequence of benefits, interface examples, and real-world imagery.

The purchase decision

Introduce a new way to buy ZOLEO.

The new product detail page builds on the earlier product presentation with a dedicated purchase experience.

The objectiveMake Add to Cart the primary action and reduce uncertainty around the device, plan, and accessories.

Design direction. The PDP direction brings features, specifications, social proof, and box contents into the purchase decision. Plan comparison and relevant accessories support that decision without losing focus on the primary action.
