01 / Challenge & role

Support a business transition with a coherent shopping experience.

The project brought Roadpost and BlueCosmo together while moving from Magento to BigCommerce. The design challenge was to serve recreational and professional customers in one storefront, preserve useful shopping patterns, and address friction in the existing experience.

The business context

Storefront consolidation and a platform change, with mobile a priority because it accounts for most customer use.

My contribution

I reviewed the existing experience, shortlisted suitable themes, evaluated five in depth, and designed wireframes and the new website experience.

What shipped

The new BlueCosmo storefront launched on BigCommerce in April 2026, using Halo as its theme foundation.

My work covered research and design within the wider consolidation and migration project. Halo was selected by the team following the theme evaluation.

01

Design for both customer groups.

A 2023 survey by the customer service team informed our understanding of recreational and professional buyers across Roadpost and BlueCosmo. The site review highlighted product-first content and easy access to refills, support, and accounts as strengths to preserve.

02

Evaluate themes against requirements, beyond appearance.

I reviewed a broader range of themes and shortlisted five for deeper research. The evaluation considered product discovery, mobile navigation, purchase flows, integrations, maintainability, and future business needs.

The team selected Halo. My evaluation identified flexible layouts and product-page customization as strengths, while flagging additional B2B customization and limitations in persistent product comparison.

03

Make mobile shopping a core design consideration.

With most customers using mobile, smaller-screen browsing and purchase flows were central to the design. The earlier review also identified performance and accessibility issues, which informed the requirements rather than being treated as visual finishing touches.

Competitive UX comparison

I compared four satellite-commerce websites to review navigation, product discovery, purchase flows, and mobile usability. The analysis documented strengths and opportunities alongside the existing-site review and theme evaluation.

The comparison covered Satellite Phone Store, Canada Satellite, SatPhoneStore, and Outfitter Satellite. It also reviewed product information, ratings and reviews, calls to action, help content, checkout, and accessibility considerations.

Source: Andrea-Helena Einlo, Competitor Analysis for UX Design, prepared for BlueCosmo.

Theme evaluation

The five shortlisted themes were Camden B2B, Beautify, Halo Electronics, Nebula, and Vault. The comparison covered navigation, filtering, image zoom, stock status, checkout, integrations, and SEO/accessibility support.

What the site review identified

  • Missing quick-view and quick-add options in listings
  • Long product-page scrolling and purchase actions moving out of view
  • Performance, contrast, and labelling issues

Halo trade-offs documented at selection

  • Extra customization for B2B features
  • Product comparisons that did not persist between pages
  • An additional service cost for a global notice

Customer context and strengths to retain

The 2023 customer service survey covered both customer bases, including differences in personal versus work use and buying frequency. The review also identified clean layouts, strong imagery, clear calls to action, and quick access to refills, support, and accounts as strengths to carry forward.

Launch requirements

The requirements included a flexible homepage, improved product discovery, clear pricing and stock information, cross-sell opportunities, responsive layouts, and a streamlined checkout. Brand consistency, maintainability, integrations, scalability, SEO, and accessibility informed the evaluation.

Halo’s design strengths

The project research highlighted a professional layout with strong visual appeal, extensive theme customization, and flexible product-page customization. Those advantages were considered alongside the documented trade-offs.

Mobile layout decisions

The final homepage uses a compact header, stacked sections, and horizontally browsable product categories. The product view brings imagery, information, and purchase controls into a single-column layout.

These were project research findings and requirements. They do not establish that every issue was resolved or every feature shipped.

05 / Outcomes & measurement

A launched storefront. Impact to assess.

Project outcome: the consolidated BlueCosmo storefront launched on BigCommerce in April 2026. My contribution included theme research, wireframes, and desktop and mobile page design.

Impact measurement: post-launch uplift is not yet documented here. A suggested next step is to compare results with the pre-launch baseline, segmented by device and customer journey.

Business questionSuggested evidence to review
Is purchasing easier on mobile?Mobile conversion, Add to Cart rate, and checkout completion
Can customers find relevant products?Search-to-product and listing-to-product progression
Did page performance and accessibility improve?Page performance and accessibility checks on key shopping pages

Platform, design, and storefront consolidation changed together. Any comparison should account for traffic mix, promotions, and seasonality before attributing an uplift to design.
