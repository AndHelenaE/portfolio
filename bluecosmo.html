01 / Challenge & role

Support a business transition with a coherent shopping experience.

The project brought Roadpost and BlueCosmo together while moving from Magento to BigCommerce. The design challenge was to serve recreational and professional customers in one storefront, preserve useful shopping patterns, and address friction in the existing experience.

My work covered research and design within the wider consolidation and migration project.

02 / Research & decisions

Turn research into practical design decisions.

01

Design for both customer groups.

A 2023 survey by the customer service team informed our understanding of recreational and professional buyers across Roadpost and BlueCosmo. The site review highlighted product-first content and easy access to refills, support, and accounts as strengths to preserve.

02

Evaluate themes against requirements, beyond appearance.

I reviewed a broader range of themes and shortlisted five for deeper research. The evaluation considered product discovery, mobile navigation, purchase flows, integrations, maintainability, and future business needs.

The team selected Halo Electronics following the evaluation. The detailed comparison below documents its strengths and trade-offs.

03

Make mobile shopping a core design consideration.

With most customers using mobile, smaller-screen browsing and purchase flows were central to the design. The earlier review also identified performance and accessibility issues, which informed the requirements rather than being treated as visual finishing touches.

Explore competitive research and theme evaluation

Competitive UX comparison

I compared four satellite-commerce websites to review navigation, product discovery, purchase flows, and mobile usability. The analysis documented strengths and opportunities alongside the existing-site review and theme evaluation.

The comparison covered Satellite Phone Store, Canada Satellite, SatPhoneStore, and Outfitter Satellite. It also reviewed product information, ratings and reviews, calls to action, help content, checkout, and accessibility considerations.

Source: Andrea-Helena Einlo, Competitor Analysis for UX Design, prepared for BlueCosmo.

Theme evaluation

The five shortlisted themes were Camden B2B, Beautify, Halo Electronics, Nebula, and Vault. The comparison covered navigation, filtering, image zoom, stock status, checkout, integrations, and SEO/accessibility support.

What the site review identified

  • Missing quick-view and quick-add options in listings
  • Long product-page scrolling and purchase actions moving out of view
  • Performance, contrast, and labelling issues

Halo Electronics trade-offs at selection

  • Extra customization for B2B features
  • Product comparisons that did not persist between pages
  • An additional service cost for a global notice

Customer context and strengths to retain

The survey highlighted differences in personal versus work use and buying frequency. Clean layouts, strong imagery, and clear calls to action were additional strengths identified in the site review.

Launch requirements

The requirements included a flexible homepage, improved product discovery, clear pricing and stock information, cross-sell opportunities, responsive layouts, and a streamlined checkout. Brand consistency, maintainability, integrations, scalability, SEO, and accessibility informed the evaluation.

Halo Electronics design strengths

The evaluation highlighted a professional layout, extensive theme customization, and flexible product-page layouts as strengths.

These were project research findings and requirements. They do not establish that every issue was resolved or every feature shipped.

04 / Design evolution

Early wireframes

Objective Explore page structure, information priorities, and next steps across the shopping journey.

The site review and theme requirements informed these first low-fidelity wireframes. I explored alternative homepage, search, and product-page layouts before applying the final visual direction.

The homepage explored the lead message, discovery routes, promotions, and supporting content. Search brought filters, sorting, and product cards together. Product pages placed imagery and purchase options above supporting detail and related products; the cart grouped items and order totals with recommendations and service information.

Final designs

Bringing the storefront together.

The final designs bring page structure, the shared visual foundations, and Halo Electronics together across browsing and purchase pages on BigCommerce. Switch between desktop and mobile to explore the new designs. The earlier views show desktop; promotional content differs between captures.

Homepage

Objective

Help recreational and professional customers find products, airtime options, and relevant shopping routes.

Design response

  • Organize product discovery, airtime, and promotional content while retaining the product-first focus identified in the site review.
  • Adapt the mobile layout with a compact header, stacked sections, and horizontally browsable product categories.

Product page

Objective

Help shoppers evaluate the product and move toward Add to Cart with relevant detail and reassurance.

Design response

  • Group product imagery, pricing, reviews, and Add to Cart at the top.
  • Provide tabbed details, related accessories, and customer reviews further down the page.
  • Bring imagery, information, and purchase controls into a single-column mobile layout.

Both examples show the Iridium 9555, so the placement of product information and purchase controls can be compared directly.

Product discovery

Objective

Help shoppers narrow their options and find a relevant satellite phone.

Design response

  • Bring filters, sorting, ratings, and product options together in the new category listing.
  • Use a compact Filters & Sort control on mobile.

These examples show different discovery entry points: earlier search results and a new satellite-phone category listing.
